Margin stands on open models and open-source libraries. Thank you to everyone who made and shared them. Each keeps its own license, listed below.

Speech and speaker models aren't bundled with Margin. You download the ones you choose, and they come under the licenses of their authors. Several are licensed CC-BY-4.0, which asks for attribution; this page is where Margin gives it.

Entries marked to confirm haven't yet been checked against the current model card or repository. Always treat the original source as authoritative.
